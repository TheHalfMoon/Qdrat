# Qudra Domain Experiences

Status: FOUNDER-DIRECTED PRODUCT PLAN

Qudra is one local decision system expressed through many domain experiences. These are not separate AI products or independent engines. Every experience must reuse the same DecisionCase, OptionSet, Capability Graph, CapabilityPlan, Company Brain, Rules, Flow, approval, audit and outcome-learning contracts.

## 1. Universal experiences

### Qudra Front Door
One intake point for tasks, enquiries, exceptions, signals, uploaded documents, meeting actions, forms and explicit user questions. It resolves subject objects, intent, urgency, owner candidates, evidence and next action.

### Qudra Business Inbox
One cross-domain queue for Needs Decision, Needs Approval, Needs Response, At Risk, Opportunity, Exception, Waiting, Delegated and Watching.

### Qudra Next Best Action
For any object, show the best currently feasible actions after hard constraints and policy filtering. Always allow evidence inspection and alternative options.

### Qudra Decision Room
Collaborative, evidence-backed decision workspace for material choices. Supports assumptions, alternatives, scenarios, stakeholder impact, approvals, dissent, final decision and outcome review.

### Qudra What-If
Recompute the Decision Frontier under changed assumptions, priorities, budgets, dates, capacity or external conditions without mutating production state.

### Qudra Negotiator
Build negotiation options inside explicit authority limits:
- target;
- walk-away boundary;
- concession ladder;
- give/get pairs;
- expiration;
- approval threshold;
- BATNA/reference option;
- non-price terms;
- relationship impact.

Qudra may prepare and compare negotiation positions. It may only execute externally when the action and autonomy policy explicitly permit it.

### Qudra Commitment Tracker
Detect promises and obligations in contracts, meetings, messages, cases and approvals. Convert them into linked commitments with owner, due date, evidence, dependency and escalation.

### Qudra Escalation Advisor
Determine whether an item should remain with the current owner, be reassigned, escalated, split, or converted into an incident/case/decision. Escalation reasons must be inspectable.

### Qudra Evidence Collector
When a decision is under-informed, generate a bounded evidence-acquisition plan using local connectors, Company Brain, local browser research or human questions.

### Qudra Playbook Builder
Promote repeated successful decision patterns into deterministic Rules/Flow after review, simulation and versioned publication.

## 2. Work and project experiences

### Qudra Task Intake
For new work, propose:
- normalized title/type;
- priority;
- owner/team;
- deadline;
- required skills;
- dependencies;
- suggested subtasks;
- effort band;
- related customer/project/service;
- next action.

### Qudra Scheduler
Generate feasible schedules under:
- task dependencies;
- milestones;
- skills;
- calendars;
- leave;
- working hours;
- resource capacity;
- location/time-zone constraints;
- SLA/deadlines;
- budget;
- change-freeze windows.

Show fastest, lowest-risk, lowest-cost and most-resilient schedules when distinct.

### Qudra Resource Optimizer
Compare assignments of people, equipment, rooms, budget and specialist capacity. Preserve hard employment/policy constraints and explain overload or bottleneck trade-offs.

### Qudra Project Rescue
When a project is slipping, build materially different recovery plans such as:
- scope reduction;
- resource increase;
- sequencing change;
- milestone renegotiation;
- parallelization;
- staged delivery;
- dependency removal;
- customer expectation reset.

For each plan show time, cost, risk, capacity, customer impact and reversibility.

### Qudra Portfolio Optimizer
Prioritize projects/initiatives under capacity, budget, strategic objectives, dependencies and mandatory obligations. Expose portfolio trade-offs rather than one hidden score.

### Qudra Meeting-to-Decision
Convert meetings into decisions, unresolved questions, commitments, tasks and follow-up DecisionCases with transcript/source evidence.

## 3. Service and customer support experiences

### Qudra Client Enquiry
Resolve customer/account, intent, related contract/project/case/invoice/service, urgency, SLA, sentiment, unresolved history and best next action.

### Qudra Case Router
Route cases using hard ownership rules first and local decision models second. Detect duplicates, related incidents and likely escalation.

### Qudra Response Options
Offer grounded response strategies, not only drafted prose:
- answer immediately;
- request clarification;
- open specialist case;
- escalate;
- schedule call;
- create incident;
- offer approved remediation.

### Qudra SLA Rescue
When an SLA is at risk, generate feasible rescue paths including reassignment, priority raise, specialist intervention, workaround, customer communication and deadline renegotiation where contractually permitted.

### Qudra Incident Commander
For operational incidents:
- assemble impacted services/customers/assets;
- correlate recent changes;
- propose containment/rollback/forward-fix/isolation options;
- map owners;
- preserve command log;
- track decisions and recovery evidence.

No autonomous privileged remediation without the required action policy and approvals.

### Qudra Problem Finder
Cluster recurring incidents/cases, identify candidate common causes, quantify impact, and propose investigation or permanent-fix work.

## 4. CRM, sales and customer-success experiences

### Qudra Account Brief
Produce an evidence-linked local view of account health, contracts, usage, service history, projects, invoices, stakeholders, opportunities, risks and commitments.

### Qudra Customer Rescue
For at-risk accounts, generate recovery options across product/service, delivery, commercial terms, executive engagement and success plans.

### Qudra Renewal Advisor
Compare renewal options:
- standard renewal;
- multi-year;
- phased;
- upsell;
- downsell;
- service-credit remediation;
- revised scope;
- no-renewal / managed exit.

### Qudra Deal Desk
Evaluate commercial exceptions against:
- margin;
- discount policy;
- delivery capacity;
- payment risk;
- contract terms;
- strategic value;
- precedent;
- approval thresholds.

### Qudra Pricing Option Advisor
Generate policy-compliant price/package alternatives. Deterministic price calculations and floor/ceiling rules remain authoritative.

### Qudra Opportunity Next Best Action
Propose the next feasible action from real account context: research, contact, demo, proposal, technical validation, procurement support, executive sponsorship, or wait.

### Qudra Territory / Account Focus
Prioritize accounts or opportunities using transparent objectives and capacity constraints. Never infer protected/sensitive personal traits.

## 5. Finance experiences

### Qudra Cash Priority
Prioritize collections, payments and cash-preservation actions while respecting contractual obligations and approval policy.

### Qudra Collections Advisor
Compare contact/escalation/payment-plan options using invoice state, history, account value, dispute status and policy.

### Qudra Expense Exception
Explain why an expense differs from policy or historical expectation and present safe next actions: approve, request evidence, split, reclassify, reject, or escalate.

### Qudra Reconciliation Exception
Rank likely causes of unmatched or inconsistent financial records and propose evidence-driven resolution steps.

### Qudra Budget Reallocator
Compare reallocations between approved budget envelopes and show delivery, cash and strategic impacts.

### Qudra Cashflow Scenario
Run local scenarios for timing shifts, hiring, purchases, renewals, collection changes and project delays. Mark facts, assumptions and forecasts separately.

No financial posting, payment or journal execution bypasses existing finance controls.

## 6. Procurement and vendor experiences

### Qudra Purchase Advisor
Compare buy now, wait, lease, rent, internal reuse, different vendor, split order, framework agreement, and phased purchase.

### Qudra Vendor Compare
Evaluate qualified suppliers on price, lead time, service, risk, certifications, performance history, contract terms and reversibility.

### Qudra Vendor Negotiator
Prepare bounded negotiation options for price, payment terms, SLA, warranty, volume, renewal and termination rights.

### Qudra Supplier Risk
Combine contract dates, delivery performance, incidents, certifications, concentration and operational dependencies into explainable risk signals.

### Qudra Renewal / Replace Advisor
For software or supplier renewal, compare renew, renegotiate, replace, consolidate, reduce scope, or exit.

## 7. People experiences

Qudra must be especially conservative in employment-related decisions. It may support managers with job-relevant evidence, scenarios and operational planning, but consequential employment decisions remain human-controlled and policy-governed.

### Qudra Workforce Planner
Model headcount, skills, capacity, location, shift and cost scenarios.

### Qudra Shift Scheduler
Generate compliant schedules under working-time, leave, skill, coverage and fairness constraints.

### Qudra Onboarding Rescue
Detect missing dependencies across HR, IT, payroll, facilities, equipment, documents and training; propose recovery sequence.

### Qudra Offboarding Assurance
Detect unreturned assets, open access, unresolved handovers, payroll/document obligations and customer/project dependencies.

### Qudra Skills Gap
Compare required capability demand against evidence-backed skills and propose training, hiring, contracting or workload alternatives.

### Qudra Leave Impact
Show coverage and delivery consequences of leave scenarios without turning leave rights into an opaque AI score.

### Qudra Hiring Support
Help structure job-relevant evidence, interview consistency, scheduling and process completeness. Final selection decisions remain human-controlled; no protected-trait inference.

## 8. Operations experiences

### Qudra Maintenance Advisor
Prioritize maintenance using safety, downtime, service impact, warranty, failure history, parts and technician availability.

### Qudra Asset Advisor
Compare repair, replace, extend warranty, reassign or retire options.

### Qudra Facility Scheduler
Optimize rooms/equipment/resources under capacity, location, maintenance and accessibility constraints.

### Qudra Service Impact
Given a change/failure, traverse Company Twin relationships to identify likely affected services, customers, contracts and work.

### Qudra Capacity Planner
Forecast and compare infrastructure/operational capacity options using local metrics and deterministic constraints.

## 9. Trust, security and compliance experiences

### Qudra Access Review
Prioritize access reviews and explain relationships, role changes, dormant entitlements and policy conflicts. Revocation still follows normal authorization/approval.

### Qudra Policy Impact
When a policy/rule changes, identify affected people, contracts, workflows, forms, automations, integrations and reports before publication.

### Qudra Compliance Evidence
Identify missing control evidence and create bounded collection plans.

### Qudra Security Triage
Classify and prioritize security findings using technical evidence and business impact. Security enforcement remains governed by existing Trust controls.

### Qudra Change Risk
Compare rollout, canary, delay, rollback-ready and maintenance-window strategies for sensitive changes.

## 10. Executive experiences

### Qudra Operating Brief
Evidence-linked daily/weekly company brief covering customer, people, work, cash, service, risk, procurement and operations.

### Qudra Executive Decision Room
Cross-domain scenario comparison for major commitments, investments, restructures, supplier changes, product launches and strategic customers.

### Qudra Strategic Portfolio
Compare initiatives using objectives, mandatory obligations, capacity, cash, dependencies and scenario uncertainty.

### Qudra Business Pulse
Detect material deviations from expected operating patterns and explain likely drivers without conflating correlation with causation.

## 11. Decision Studio

Studio must let authorized administrators define new Qudra decision types without source forks.

A DecisionType definition should support:
- subject object types;
- triggers;
- context sources;
- hard constraints;
- objectives;
- option generators;
- allowed capabilities;
- deterministic calculations;
- local decision questions/rubrics;
- local model profile;
- scenario variables;
- approval policy;
- autonomy mode;
- outcome metrics;
- evaluation fixtures;
- UI surfaces.

Publishing a DecisionType requires versioning, validation, simulation fixtures, permission review and rollback compatibility.

## 12. Common rollout modes

Every domain experience declares one of:
- OBSERVE;
- SHADOW;
- RECOMMEND;
- PREPARE;
- EXECUTE_BOUNDED;
- HUMAN_CONTROLLED.

Promotion between modes requires domain-specific evidence. There is no global autonomous-Qudra switch.

## 13. Common UX contract

Every Qudra domain experience must reuse:
- Action Card;
- Decision Case;
- Decision Frontier;
- Compare Options;
- Evidence Drawer;
- What-If controls;
- Approval panel;
- Outcome Review;
- audit timeline.

Do not create a different AI UX per suite.

## 14. Non-goals

Qudra does not:
- replace deterministic accounting/payroll calculations with model judgement;
- create employment authority from model confidence;
- silently negotiate outside approved limits;
- infer protected/sensitive traits;
- make cloud inference mandatory;
- create a second workflow, identity, audit or permission system;
- execute a recommendation merely because it ranked first.
