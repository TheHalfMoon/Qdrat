# Gate plan and measurable exits

All gates are currently unverified. G0 is OPEN; all successors are LOCKED by evidence dependencies, not by calendar dates. Tasks inside a gate form an ordered frontier. Independent gates may progress concurrently only when their shared contracts are accepted and change ownership does not overlap. No date estimate substitutes for evidence.

G10 qualifies the first production People release after G5; it deliberately does not wait for Studio, CRM or AI. Optional suites require their own gate before activation. G9 follows Service/Work because transcript-to-task/case needs their real APIs. G12 is admission/specification work for later domains, not a claim that a general ledger or every country is implemented.
